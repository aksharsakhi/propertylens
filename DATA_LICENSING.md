# Data Licensing Guidelines & Compliance Framework

## Compliance Principles
1. **No Proprietary Portal Scraping:** PropertyLens does not scrape commercial listing sites (99acres, Housing.com, MagicBricks) in violation of terms of service.
2. **Open Database Compliance:** All OpenStreetMap data ingestion complies with ODbL requirements. Mandatory attribution is displayed on maps and reports ("© OpenStreetMap contributors").
3. **Government Open Data:** Data sourced from CPCB and India OGD Portals follows the Open Government Data License India.
4. **Restricted Source Protocol:** Spatial layers requiring commercial licenses (e.g. proprietary TNGIS vector datasets) are marked as `REQUIRES_PERMISSION` and replaced with open geospatial alternatives in production.
