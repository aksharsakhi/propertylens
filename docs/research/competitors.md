# Competitor Landscape Analysis

## Overview
This document evaluates current real estate decision tools, locality rating platforms, and location intelligence startups operating in India.

## Evaluated Competitors

### 1. Myly
- **Focus:** Micro-neighbourhood ratings and social safety metrics.
- **Strengths:** User-generated community reviews and qualitative locality ratings.
- **Weaknesses:** Highly subjective, lacks spatial hydrological/flood analysis, limited to broad locality averages rather than property-level metrics.

### 2. LokaScore
- **Focus:** Locality liveability indexing and urban metrics.
- **Strengths:** Clean aggregate scores for major localities.
- **Weaknesses:** Locality-level only (not property address specific), static scoring without user preference weighting, proprietary non-verifiable data sources.

### 3. TalkingLands
- **Focus:** 3D spatial visualization and plot layout discovery for real estate developers.
- **Strengths:** High-fidelity 3D visualization and developer sales enablement.
- **Weaknesses:** Focused on developer marketing rather than consumer due-diligence; lacks independent risk analysis and evidence links.

### 4. Nexscore / TwoGets
- **Focus:** Predictive property valuations and general investment ratings.
- **Strengths:** Algorithmic yield and price trend projections.
- **Weaknesses:** Ignores localized infrastructure hazards (e.g., Chennai low-lying flood basins, municipal water grid boundaries); prone to optimistic bias.
