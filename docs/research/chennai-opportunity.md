# Chennai Market Opportunity & Hyper-Local Dynamics

## Why Chennai as the First Market?
Chennai presents an ideal market for PropertyLens' decision-first architecture:
1. **Severe Inundation & Hydrological Vulnerability:** Chennai experiences intense Northeast Monsoon rain events. Suburbs like Velachery, Pallikaranai, Mudichur, Medavakkam, and Perumbakkam suffer regular inundation, making flood intelligence paramount.
2. **Rapid Infrastructure Expansion:** The CMRL Phase 2 expansion (118.9 km across 3 corridors) and Peripheral Ring Road projects are dramatically altering transit accessibility.
3. **Water Supply Disparities:** Neighbourhoods vary wildly between piped Metrowater supply and 100% private tanker dependence.
4. **Tech Corridor Concentration:** Over 60% of modern residential decisions in South Chennai occur along OMR, ECR, GST Road, and Mount-Poonamallee Road, driven by IT workers requiring predictable commuting times.

## Target Neighbourhood Coverage (Phase 1 MVP)
The MVP targets 20+ key neighbourhoods with hyper-local spatial coverage:
- South Chennai / IT Corridor: Velachery, Adyar, Taramani, Perungudi, OMR, Thoraipakkam, Sholinganallur, Besant Nagar, Palavakkam.
- Central & West Chennai: Guindy, Nungambakkam, Anna Nagar, Porur, Saidapet, Kodambakkam, Mylapore.
- Southern Suburbs: Pallikaranai, Medavakkam, Tambaram, Chromepet, Kelambakkam.
