# PropertyLens Product Positioning & Core Principles

## Core Tagline
**"Know the property before you commit."**

## Core Value Proposition
PropertyLens is an AI-powered Property Intelligence & Decision Platform built to solve information asymmetry in Indian residential real estate. Instead of generating property listing leads or outputting generic locality ratings, PropertyLens synthesizes spatial hydrological data, transit connectivity, water security, civic infrastructure, and price benchmarks into an evidence-backed decision report.

## The 9 Core Differentiators
1. **Decision-First, Not Listing-First:** Answers "Should I consider this property?" rather than "Here are 50 listings."
2. **Property-Level Resolution:** Analyzes exact lat-long coordinates rather than broad pincode averages.
3. **Evidence-Backed Explanations:** Every metric links directly to source names, observation dates, methodologies, and limitations.
4. **Transparent Sources & Freshness:** Displays exact data update timestamps and source health status.
5. **Confidence Scoring:** Explicitly classifies data as HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, or UNKNOWN.
6. **"Things to Verify" Due-Diligence Checklist:** Provides actionable pre-token verification steps for buyers/renters.
7. **Personalized Preference Weighting:** Re-calculates property scores based on user priorities (commute, budget, flood safety, schools).
8. **Consumer-Friendly Language:** Demystifies complex urban hydrology and civic planning terminology.
9. **Chennai-First Depth:** Delivers hyper-local hydrological and transit accuracy before expanding to other Indian metros.

## Product Disclaimer Boundary
PropertyLens provides informational decision support. It does not replace legal, structural engineering, environmental, or professional due diligence.
