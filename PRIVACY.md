# Privacy Policy & Data Minimization

## Policy Summary
PropertyLens is built with data minimization at its core:
1. **No Exact Address Storage Without Consent:** Searches performed by anonymous users are evaluated dynamically without persisting exact house numbers or personal identifiers.
2. **Preference Isolation:** User priorities (commute workplace, budget, family preferences) are stored in session state or local storage unless explicitly saved into a registered user account.
3. **No Third-Party Tracker Scraping:** We do not trade or sell search history or location queries to real estate brokers or lead brokers.
4. **Compliance:** Designed in alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
